TEAM 1 — FRONTEND REQUIREMENTS

Goal:
Repair the CampusConnect frontend and make it usable on desktop, tablet and mobile.

Must verify:
1. All navigation links point to valid sections.
2. Every form field has a label.
3. Required fields are clearly handled.
4. Cards remain readable on small screens.
5. Complaint table can be viewed without breaking the page.
6. Buttons are visible and clickable.
7. No horizontal overflow on a 360px-wide screen.
8. Heading hierarchy is sensible.
9. Color/status labels are understandable.
10. Explain at least 3 frontend problems found.

Deliver:
- corrected index.html
- corrected style.css
- frontend investigation sheet
- evidence classification
- 5-minute explanation
